# AgriChain AI 🌾⛓️

AgriChain AI is an intelligent agricultural supply chain and farm management platform powered by AI and blockchain transparency.

## Overview
- **AI-Powered Diagnostics & Insights**: Crop health monitoring, yield prediction, and smart advisory.
- **Supply Chain Traceability**: Transparent tracking from farm to consumer.
- **Smart Contracts & Fair Trade**: Direct, reliable marketplace connections for farmers and buyers.
